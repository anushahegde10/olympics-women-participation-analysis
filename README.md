# Celebrating Women in Olympics

An analytical deep-dive into 116 years of women's participation in the 
Olympic Games from 23 athletes in 1900 to 45.5% of all participants 
in 2016, using Power BI.

## Project Overview

The Olympic dataset contains the complete history of all athletes across 
all games. This project focuses on a specific analytical question: how has 
women's participation evolved across countries, sports and age groups?

Inspired by the performance of women athletes at **Tokyo Olympics 2020**.

## Key Insights

- Female participation grew from **0.6% in 1906 to 45.5% in 2016** — 
  a 7,062% increase
- **6 countries** had female athletes in 1900 **200 countries** by 2016
- **33,981 female athletes** across 207 countries and 53 sport events
- **Athletics** most participated sport with 11,666 female athletes
- **USA** leads total female performances with 5,533
- Most athletes aged **21-25** ; same group that wins most medals
- **69%** of athletes participated in only one Olympics
- Youngest medal winner at just **11 years old** in 1928
